# Confidentiality Statement Slide

## Overview
A legal/notice slide included in every client-facing deck. Always placed near the start (after index) or end of the deck. Three layout variants differing in text placement and visual weight.

## Template file
`templates/powerpoint/presentations-skill.pptx`

---

## Variants

### Variant 1 — Confidentiality / Bottom Left (Slide 10)
Title and body anchored to the bottom-left of the slide. Light background with branded layout image.

| Shape name | Content |
|---|---|
| `confidentiality-title` | "Confidentiality Statement" (do not change) |
| `confidentiality-body` | Full legal text |

Positions: title @(88, 692) 1210×83 · body @(88, 808) 1210×97

---

### Variant 2 — Confidentiality / Centered (Slide 11)
Title and body centered horizontally on the slide. More balanced, used in shorter or internally focused decks.

| Shape name | Content |

### Variant 3 — Confidentiality / Full-Width (Slide 12)

## Standard body text
```
This document contains proprietary and confidential information of Nagarro.
All materials herein are protected and may not be reproduced or distributed
without the prior written consent of Nagarro.
```
Replace only if the client's NDA requires specific language.

---

## When to Use
- Include in every external client-facing deck — mandatory.
- Placement: after the Index and before the Executive Summary (standard position).
- For internal decks, may be omitted or placed at the end.

## Usage Rules
- Never change the title text from "Confidentiality Statement".
- Use Variant 1 (bottom-left) as the default.
- Use Variant 3 (full-width) for high-stakes proposals where the notice should be prominent.
