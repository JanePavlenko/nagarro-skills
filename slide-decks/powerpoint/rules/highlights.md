## When to Use
- Use near client testimonials, near the Our Partners section, or as a standalone impact slide before a key argument.
- One highlights slide per section maximum — overuse dilutes impact.
- **Quote + Vertical Image** is preferred for genuine client testimonials when a portrait is available.
- **Quote, Centered / Dark** is the default when no photo is available.
- Mint variants for Nagarro brand/positioning statements; Yellow variant rarely.

## Usage Rules
- Always begin `quote` text with an opening curly-quote character (`"`).
- Attribution format: `— First Last, Role / Company`.
- Keep quote text short — **~55 characters max** to fit 3 lines at 96pt without overflow.
- Never leave placeholder quote text in the final output.
