# Cover Slide

## Overview
The opening slide of every deck. Always first. Uses a dark background with the Nagarro branded layout image baked in (logo, footer separator).

## Template file
`templates/powerpoint/presentations-skill.pptx`

## Canvas
2000 × 1125 px

---

## Variants

### Variant 1 — Cover / Standard (Slide 01)
- Title centered in the left two-thirds, vertically mid-slide
- Subtitle directly below the title
- Use for: most standard decks

| Shape name | Content | Position |
|---|---|---|
| `cover-title` | Deck title | @(88, 454) 1366×133 |
| `cover-subtitle` | Tagline or project name | @(88, 621) 1365×50 |

### Variant 2 — Cover / Large Title (Slide 02)
- Very large title filling most of the slide area (top-to-bottom)
- Subtitle sits bottom-right
- Use for: short punchy titles, keynote-style openers

| Shape name | Content | Position |
|---|---|---|
| `cover-title` | Deck title (1–3 words) | @(88, 191) 1447×533 |
| `cover-subtitle` | Subtitle or date | @(1344, 910) 569×50 |

---

## Footer shapes (both variants)
| Shape name | Content |
|---|---|
| `Digital Design Strategy` | Service line (e.g. "Digital Design Strategy") |
| `Project Name` | Client or project name |
| `Month Year` | Presentation date (e.g. "May 2026") |
| `2026` | Copyright year (pre-filled "© 2026") |

---

## When to Use
- Always the first slide of every deck — no exceptions.
- Choose Variant 1 (Slide 01) for titles longer than 4 words.
- Choose Variant 2 (Slide 02) for short, impactful titles (≤3 words).

## Usage Rules
- Never use a light background for the cover.
- Populate all four footer shapes; do not leave "Project Name" or "Month Year" as placeholders.
- Do not add extra shapes — the Layout image carries logo and decorative elements.
