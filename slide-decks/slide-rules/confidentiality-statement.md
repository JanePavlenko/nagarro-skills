# Confidentiality Statement Slide

## Overview
Legal slide presenting the confidentiality notice. The copy on this slide is fixed and should not change significantly — treat it as boilerplate. All variants are light by default.

## Light / Dark Mode
All variants support light and dark mode. Mode can be switched per slide via variables.

## Header & Footer
Information not confirmed — verify in Figma before use.

## Variants

### Confidentiality Statement - Title + Text, Bottom Left / Light
- Title and body text are both aligned to the bottom left of the frame
- Standard text size
- Text alignment: left

### Confidentiality Statement - Title + Text, Centered / Light
- Same text size as the bottom-left variant
- All elements are centered both vertically and horizontally in the frame
- Text alignment: center (unlike most other slides which use left-aligned text)

### Confidentiality Statement - Large Title Top, Text Bottom Left / Light
- Title is larger and heavier than the body text, aligned to the top left
- Body text occupies approximately 5 out of 12 columns, aligned to the bottom left
- Text alignment: left

## When to Use
- Use in every deck — internal and external — it is one slide and easy to remove if not needed
- Place immediately after the Index (third slide in the deck, part of the intro block)
- Choose variant based on surrounding slides:
  - **Bottom Left**: default — suits most deck styles
  - **Centered**: use when the surrounding slides are also center-aligned or the deck has a more symmetrical layout
  - **Large Title Top, Text Bottom Left**: use when you want the legal content to feel more considered and less like fine print

## Usage Rules
- Do not alter the copy on this slide — it is a legal statement
- Typically placed at the beginning or end of the deck
- Choose the variant that best fits the visual flow of the surrounding slides
