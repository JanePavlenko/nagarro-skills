# A Note From Us Slide

## Overview
A personal message from the Nagarro team — typically a short, warm introduction or closing thought. All variants are light by default.

## Light / Dark Mode
All variants support light and dark mode. Mode can be switched per slide via variables.

## Header & Footer
Information not confirmed — verify in Figma before use.

## Variants

### A Note From Us - 3 Col, Title as First Col / Light
- Layout uses 3 columns
- The first column is the slide title
- The remaining two columns hold the message copy
- Everything centered vertically

### A Note From Us - 2 Col / Light
- Layout uses 2 columns
- Title and copy distributed across the two columns

### A Note From Us - Copy Bottom Left / Light
- Copy aligned to the bottom left of the frame
- Suitable for a short, impactful message

### A Note From Us - Title Top, Text Bottom Left / Light
- Title aligned to the top left
- Body text aligned to the bottom left

### A Note From Us - Copy Bottom Left + Team Photo / Light
- Same layout as "Copy Bottom Left"
- A photo of the Nagarro team is placed to the right, filling the remaining space
- Use when a human/team visual adds value to the message

## When to Use
- Use when the deck needs a human, personal touch — typically in proposals and pitches where relationship matters
- Triggered by brief content such as: "personal message", "a word from us", "introduction from the team", "closing note", "message from leadership"
- Position: 5th slide in the deck, immediately after the Executive Summary — one slide only
- Always comes after the Executive Summary, never before it
- Do not use in short technical decks or purely informational presentations where a personal note would feel out of place
- Choose variant based on copy length and whether a visual is available:
  - **3 Col, Title as First Col**: use for medium-length messages — the title column gives the slide gravitas
  - **2 Col**: use for a balanced, readable layout when the message is a few short paragraphs
  - **Copy Bottom Left**: use for very short, impactful messages (2–4 lines)
  - **Title Top, Text Bottom Left**: use when the title is a standalone statement and the body is a brief follow-through
  - **Copy Bottom Left + Team Photo**: use whenever a team photo is available — adds warmth and credibility

## Usage Rules
- Copy should be personal and concise — avoid corporate language
- The team photo variant (v5) requires an image from the Images - References page (People or team section)
- Typically placed near the beginning of the deck as an introduction, or near the end as a closing note
