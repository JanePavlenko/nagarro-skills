# Assumptions Slide

## Overview
Lists the assumptions made in the context of a proposal or project. All variants are light by default.

## Light / Dark Mode
All variants support light and dark mode. Mode can be switched per slide via variables.

## Header & Footer
Information not confirmed — verify in Figma before use.

## Variants

### Assumptions - 3 Col, Title as First Col / Light
- 3-column layout where the **slide title occupies the entire first column** (large bold text, vertically centered on the left)
- The middle and right columns each hold a bullet list of assumptions
- Use when the assumptions list is long enough to fill 2 columns and you want the title to have strong visual weight on the left

### Assumptions - 3 Col, Title Above / Light
- Slide title sits above all 3 columns as a full-width heading
- Below the title: 3 equal columns each holding a bullet list of assumptions
- Use when you have a long list that fills 3 columns and the title should read before the content

### Assumptions - 2 Col, Title Above / Light
- Slide title sits above 2 equal columns as a full-width heading
- Below the title: 2 columns each holding a bullet list of assumptions
- Use when the assumptions list is shorter and doesn't need 3 columns

### Assumptions - 3 Col, Title Top + Text Bottom / Light
- Large slide title aligned to the top left, taking up roughly the top half of the content area
- A clear vertical gap separates the title from the content below
- 3 columns of bullet-point assumptions fill the bottom portion of the slide
- Use when the title needs to read as a strong statement before the reader reaches the list — creates more visual breathing room than the "Title Above" variant

## When to Use
- Use in proposals and SOW responses where the commercial offer depends on conditions being true
- Triggered by brief content such as: "assumptions", "prerequisites", "conditions", "dependencies", "we assume"
- Place toward the end of the deck, after the solution/approach and before the Outro
- Not needed in capability overviews, recruitment decks, or purely informational presentations
- Choose variant based on list length:
  - **3 Col, Title as First Col**: use for medium lists (7–12 items) — the title column adds visual balance
  - **3 Col, Title Above**: use for long lists (10+ items) that need all 3 columns for content
  - **2 Col, Title Above**: use for short lists (up to 6 items)
  - **3 Col, Title Top + Text Bottom**: use when the title is a strong framing statement and the list should feel subordinate to it

## Usage Rules
- List only genuine assumptions — items that, if wrong, would change the scope or pricing
- Prefer the 3-column variants for longer lists; use the 2-column variant for 6 or fewer items
- Typically placed toward the end of the deck, before the outro
