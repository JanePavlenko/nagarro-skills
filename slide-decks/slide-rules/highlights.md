# Highlights Slide

## Overview
A quote or highlight slide — typically used to showcase a key statement, client quote, or impactful metric. Two groups: quote-only (6 variants) and quote with image (2 variants). 8 variants total.

## Light / Dark Mode
All variants support light and dark mode. Mode can be switched per slide via variables. The dark/light appearance adapts automatically via the variable system — do not use separate dark-frame versions.

Naming convention for color-themed variants: `/ Light|Dark / ColorTheme`

## Group 1 — Quote Only (no image)

These 6 variants contain only the quote text and the name/role of the person who said it.

### v1 — Highlights - Quote, Bottom Left / Light
- Quote and attribution aligned to the bottom left
- Text alignment: left
- Background: white (`Colors/Neutral/White`)

### v2 — Highlights - Quote, Centered / Light

## When to Use
- Use when a single quote, stat, or statement deserves its own slide to land with full impact
- Triggered by content such as: a client testimonial, a striking metric ("99% satisfaction"), a bold claim, or a key message that needs to breathe
- Default placement: alongside or immediately after a testimonials/partners section — the quote reinforces the social proof already on screen
- Can also be used as a transition beat between chapters or near the end of a pitch to leave a strong impression
- One per section maximum — do not cluster quote slides
- Choose variant based on available assets and desired tone:
  - **Bottom Left / Light (v1)**: default for most quotes — grounded and professional
  - **Centered / Light (v2)**: use for very short quotes or single-sentence stats that benefit from symmetry
  - **Centered / Light / Mint (v3)**: use for client success stories or a fresh, positive tone
  - **Centered / Light / Yellow (v4)**: use sparingly for high-energy moments, awards, or recognition
  - **Centered / Dark (v5)**: use when surrounding slides are dark, or the quote is weighty and serious
  - **Centered / Dark / Mint (v6)**: use for a premium, confident feel in dark-themed sections
  - **Quote + Vertical Image (v7)**: use when a portrait photo of the person quoted is available
  - **Quote + Squared Image (v8)**: use when a square-cropped photo or logo is available

## Usage Rules
- Always include an attribution (name + role/company) below the quote
- For client quotes, prefer the image variants if a photo is available
- Image variants: source photos from the Images - References page (People section)
- Use the Mint or Yellow color variants to add visual variety — do not use them back-to-back
- Do not use quote slides back-to-back — use sparingly for maximum impact
