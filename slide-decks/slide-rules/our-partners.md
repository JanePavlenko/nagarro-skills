# Our Partners Slide

## Overview
Showcases Nagarro's partner network, client logos, past projects, and testimonials. The most varied section — 13 slide templates plus 2 infographic slides. Mix of light and dark variants.

## Light / Dark Mode
All variants support light and dark mode. Mode can be switched per slide via variables. The dark/light appearance adapts automatically via the variable system.

## When to Use
- Use to build credibility through proof — client logos, past projects, and testimonials
- Typically placed after the About/Why Nagarro sections and before the solution or team sections
- Not every variant is needed in every deck — select based on what proof is available and what the audience values:
  - **Logo Grid variants**: use when the breadth of the client base is the proof point (many recognisable logos)
  - **Logos by Industry**: use when the audience cares about Nagarro's specific industry experience
  - **Middle Eastern Logos**: use for decks targeting Middle Eastern clients or markets
  - **Logos + Services + Tech**: use for technical audiences who want to see both client names and the tech stack Nagarro has used
  - **Project Showcase (×5)**: use when a specific past project is directly relevant to the prospect's situation — one slide per project, use 1–3 maximum per deck
  - **Co-Branding**: use only when Nagarro and a named partner are jointly presenting or bidding — requires both logos
  - **Testimonials**: use when direct client voice is the strongest proof available — ideally from a client the prospect will recognise
  - **Infographics (Line/Bar Graph)**: use to show growth trends or quantitative impact — place within the Our Partners section

## Usage Rules
- For logo slides: source logos from the Local Components → Partner Logos section
- For project showcase slides: replace the background image with a relevant photo from Images - References
- The Co-Branding slide requires both the client's logo and the Nagarro logo — ensure both are available before using
- Testimonial slide: maximum 8 testimonials (4 per column) — do not exceed this
- Infographic slides are typically placed within the Our Partners section of the deck
