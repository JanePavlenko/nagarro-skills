## 1. Who Nagarro is, in one line

A global digital engineering company that helps enterprises become **fluidic** — connected, adaptive, human-centric, AI-augmented — through digital product engineering, AI and data services, and design-led transformation.

Not a body-shop. Not a pure consultancy. Engineering with a point of view.

## 2. Big-picture frames (use these as the "north" of any campaign)

| Frame | What it means | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| **Fluidic Enterprise** | Nagarro's core vision: organizations that move, learn, and adapt as one. Connected, adaptive structures + human leadership + AI. | Senior audiences, strategy posts, RFP narratives, transformation pitches. |
| **Fluidic Intelligence** | Nagarro's AI-transformation framework — connecting people, processes, and data so the enterprise can think and adapt. | AI services posts, platform launches, AI-readiness content. |
| **Thinking Breakthroughs** | The way Nagarro frames its problem-solving stance — bold, original thinking applied to real engineering problems. | Thought-leadership pieces, founder/leader quotes, conference moments. |
| **CARING mindset** | Nagarro's internal culture acronym. Drives how we work with clients (close, accountable, human). | Culture posts, recruiting, partnership announcements. Avoid in salesy promos — it reads internal. |

Use one frame per piece. Stacking all four reads like a brochure.

## 6. Audiences for external comms (who we talk to on LinkedIn / X / Reddit / campaigns)

| Audience | What they care about | How to talk to them |
|---|---|---|
| **CTOs / VPs Engineering** | Velocity, quality, modernization, AI in the SDLC, hiring leverage. | Specific. Concrete numbers. Engineering language. No fluff. |
| **CPOs / VPs Product** | Faster discovery → ship, fewer broken handoffs, design ops, system thinking. | Practitioner voice. Show the messy reality, then the fix. |
| **Heads of Design / Design Ops** | Design systems, accessibility, design-to-code, AI in design tooling. | Craft-led. Show you've actually shipped a system. |
| **Heads of AI / Data** | AI applied to real workflows, not demos; governance; ROI. | Engineering-grade honesty. Avoid AI buzzwords. |
| **C-suite (CEO/COO/CFO)** | Adaptive enterprise, risk, cost, growth. Less how, more why. | Use the fluidic frame. Strategy register. |
| **Peers / community (engineers, designers)** | Honest takes, shared problems, craft. | Practitioner-to-practitioner. Drop the corporate voice entirely. |

Default audience for a generic LinkedIn capability post = practitioners (CTO/CPO/Head of Design). C-suite frame only when the Brief asks for it.

## 8. Anti-claims (what we never say in promo)

- We do **not** claim to be the cheapest or the biggest.
- We do **not** claim AI is magic or that "AI changes everything."
- We do **not** name clients we have not been given permission to name.
- We do **not** invent metrics. If we have not been given a number, we describe the shape of the outcome, not the size.
- We do **not** position Nagarro as "just an offshore vendor" — engineering with a point of view, not staff augmentation.
- We do **not** stack jargon (avoid "synergize", "leverage", "transformational journey", "next-gen", "world-class", "best-in-class").

## 9. Hashtag and channel conventions

- **LinkedIn:** **No hashtags.** Current LinkedIn behaviour: hashtags don't drive meaningful reach and can suppress distribution. Let the copy do the work. If a specific campaign tag is genuinely needed (e.g. a co-marketed event with a partner who owns the tag), use **one**, in PascalCase, at the end — never a stack.
- **X (Twitter):** 0–2 hashtags. Tag people, not topics.
- **Reddit:** No hashtags. Subreddit norms govern. Almost never post from a brand account; post as a practitioner with context.
- **Always:** No emojis unless the post is explicitly playful/community-toned and the user opts in.
