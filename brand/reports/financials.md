| **EBITDA** | €118.7m | €134.0m | — |

| **Net profit** | €39.5m | €49.2m | −20% |
| **Basic EPS** | €3.08 | €3.69 | — |

**Context on EBITDA decline:** The drop was driven by a €15.5m revaluation loss on intra-group loans due to USD/EUR weakening (13%), and €12.4m one-off impact from new Labour Codes in India. Underlying operational performance was positive — gross margin improved by 1.8pp, cost of revenues fell as % of revenue despite revenue growth.

**Revenue vs guidance:** Original 2025 guidance was €1,020m–€1,080m. Final revenue was €999m — below target primarily due to EUR strengthening vs USD, not operational shortfalls.

### Strategy context (from CEO letter)

Nagarro's current strategy is **"Up, Across, Together"**:
- **Up** — Elevating from engineering execution to CXO-level AI advisory
- **Across** — Enabling industry teams to capture business across regions, not just historical bases
- **Together** — Enhancing cross-selling and collaboration, especially on AI topics

The company is reorganising its GBUs into 6 industry BUs + 4 capability BUs, including two AI-focused units: **"AI in Change"** (advisory, solutions, platforms) and **"AI in Run"** (testing, managed services).

## Key messages for communications use

**Strengths to highlight:**
- Near-€1bn revenue business with 18.4% revenue CAGR since 2020
- Strong client relationships — 20 clients each doing >€10m/year, average 13.2-year relationships
- Client satisfaction consistently above 90% CSAT and NPS of 65–68
- Gross margin improving: 30.4% → 32.2% (2024→2025), continuing to 31.2% in Q1 2026
- Net profit growing: +71% YoY in Q1 2026 (€19.2m vs €11.2m)
- 18,500+ professionals across 40 countries

**Context to be careful with:**
- EBITDA margin declined in 2025 — driven by FX (USD/EUR) and one-off India labour code impact, not underlying performance
- Revenue missed original 2025 guidance — also FX-driven, not demand-driven in operating terms
- Horizontal Tech and Energy sectors declining — ISV spend is under pressure from AI disruption

**Framing the story:**
Nagarro closed 2025 approaching the €1bn mark and is entering 2026 with improving margins, growing headcount, and a clear AI-era strategic direction. The business is resilient across a diverse client and industry base, with engineering excellence as its competitive moat.
